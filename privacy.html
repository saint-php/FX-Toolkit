Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Career Toolkit (“we”, “the Service”) is a browser-based resume and ID card builder. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — email address and name when you sign up (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • Usage data — counts of free resume/ID generations and premium status, stored in our database so limits work across devices.
  • Payment data — if you pay via Paystack, payment is processed by Paystack; we only store that your account is unlocked.
  • Resume / ID content — stays in your browser unless you choose to save a version. We do not upload CV text or photos to our servers by default.
  • Ads — third-party ad partners (e.g. PropellerAds) may set cookies or collect device/browser data according to their own policies when ads are shown.

2. How we use data

  • To provide login, free limits, and premium unlock.
  • To process payments and prevent abuse of free generations.
  • To show ads when you choose the “watch ads” option.

3. Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Paystack — payments (Nigeria and supported countries).
  • PropellerAds (or successor ad partner) — advertising when you opt into the ad unlock path.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We only share what is needed to operate those features.

4. Cookies

We use browser storage (localStorage) for session convenience and may allow ad partners to use cookies when ads load. You can clear site data in your browser settings.

5. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated usage/premium records by contacting us at the email below. Export or delete resume content from within the app (browser storage) at any time.

6. Contact
